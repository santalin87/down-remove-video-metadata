How to download a video from X

  1. Open the post on X (Twitter) that contains the video or GIF, tap Share, then Copy Link.
  2. Paste the link into the box above and click Fetch Video.
  3. Pick a quality from the list — the MP4 downloads straight to your device.

Fun fact: X doesn't actually store GIFs as GIFs. When someone posts a GIF, X converts it to a looping MP4 video behind the scenes — that's why right-click saving never works. This tool grabs that original MP4, so you get the exact quality X serves, usually far smaller and sharper than a converted GIF.

Which sites are supported?

X / Twitter is the focus: videos, GIFs and multi-video posts. The same box also handles YouTube, Pinterest, TikTok and most public video pages — if a page has a playable video, it can usually be fetched. Anything behind a login, paywall, or deleted post can't be downloaded, and neither can private accounts.

Downloads are yours to keep for personal use. Respect the original creator's rights — don't re-upload someone else's video as your own.

FAQ

Is the X video downloader free?

Yes, completely free with no signup and no watermark. There are no download limits for normal use.

What quality will I get?

Up to the original quality X serves for that post — often 720p or 1080p for videos. Pick the highest option in the list for the best file.

Why can't I download this post?

Usually one of three reasons: the post was deleted, the account is private or suspended, or X is temporarily rate-limiting requests. Public posts from public accounts work best — try again in a few minutes if it fails once.

Can I download an X GIF as a real GIF file?

X stores GIFs as MP4 videos, so you download an MP4. If you need an actual .gif file, run it through our MP4 to GIF tool afterwards.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser — paste the link, fetch, download. Works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can I remove metadata while downloading?

Yes — tick "Remove metadata after download", pick an editor preset, then hit Download. The video is fetched and cleaned in one step, stamped as if exported from Premiere, DaVinci or CapCut.
